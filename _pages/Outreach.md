## Arab Forum for Space Weather Science

**Founder and organizer (2026–present).** I help coordinate scientific discussions, working groups, and Arabic-language public engagement in space-weather science, with a particular focus on collaboration and research opportunities across the Arab region.

## Arabic-language eclipse education

I am preparing **الكسوف (*Al-Kusouf / The Eclipse*)**, an illustrated Arabic science book about solar eclipses, including an observing guide for the total solar eclipse of **2 August 2027**. **The manuscript is in preparation**, not a published book.
