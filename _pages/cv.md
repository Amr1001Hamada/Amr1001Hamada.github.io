## Research expertise

Far-side helioseismology; solar magnetic-field reconstruction; polarity-resolved magnetograms; machine learning and deep learning for solar observations; uncertainty quantification; active-region and coronal-hole detection; EUV/UV synoptic-map generation; space-weather applications.
